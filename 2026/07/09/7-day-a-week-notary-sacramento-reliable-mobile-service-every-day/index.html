How to Prepare for Your Weekend Notary Appointment

Preparation is the most effective way to ensure your weekend appointment is successful. When you utilize a 7 day a week notary Sacramento service, you’re often working against a tight Monday morning deadline. To avoid delays, it’s essential to have everything ready before the professional arrives at your home or office. A few simple steps can prevent the need for a follow-up visit and ensure your documents are processed immediately without procedural errors.

First, confirm that all signers have valid, government-issued photo identification. This is the most common reason for a failed notarization. Second, do not sign your documents before the notary is present. California law requires the notary to witness the signature or, in the case of an acknowledgment, have the signer personally appear to confirm they signed the document. If your document requires witnesses, such as a Will or certain Power of Attorney forms, ensure those individuals are present and have their own valid identification. Finally, review your paperwork for completeness. Ensure no pages are missing and that you know whether the document requires an Acknowledgment or a Jurat, as the notary cannot choose the act for you.
