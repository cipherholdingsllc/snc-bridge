Identification Requirements in California

Every signer must present valid, government-issued photo identification at the time of the appointment. These documents must be current or issued within the last five years. If a signer lacks valid identification, you’ll need to arrange for two credible witnesses who personally know the signer and have their own valid IDs to vouch for the signer’s identity. California law requires all notaries to verify a signer’s identity using a state-approved identification document containing a photograph, description, signature, and serial number.

  • California Driver’s License or ID card
  • United States Passport
  • Military ID card
  • Foreign Passport (must be stamped by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services)

The Role of Witnesses in After-Hours Signings

Wills, trusts, and certain healthcare directives often require additional witnesses to be present during the signing process. It’s important to understand that a notary public generally can’t serve as a witness for the same document they’re notarizing. You’ll need to arrange for disinterested third parties, people who don’t stand to benefit from the document, to be present. Having these individuals ready to go ensures that your after hours notary Sacramento visit is efficient and the documents are legally binding.
