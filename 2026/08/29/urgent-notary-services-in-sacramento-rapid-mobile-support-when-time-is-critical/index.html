California ID Requirements Checklist

The California Secretary of State maintains strict standards for identity verification in 2026. Every signer must present a valid, government-issued photo ID. The name on the ID should match the name on the document exactly to satisfy legal scrutiny. If a signer lacks valid identification, California law allows for the “Credible Witnesses” protocol. This requires two people who personally know the signer and possess their own valid IDs to swear to the signer’s identity under oath. This is a common solution in hospital settings where a patient’s ID may be misplaced or expired.

  • California Driver’s License or ID Card: Must be current or issued within the last five years.
  • U.S. Passport: A current passport is a primary and reliable form of identification.
  • Foreign Passports: These are acceptable if they are current and stamped by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services.
  • Military ID: Must be a current, valid identification card issued by any branch of the U.S. Armed Forces.
