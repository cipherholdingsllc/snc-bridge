Accepted Forms of Identification in California

California law maintains strict standards for identity verification as of August 2026. To proceed with your mobile notary Folsom service, you must present a valid form of identification. The most commonly accepted documents include a California Driver’s License, a California ID card, or a United States Passport. Other options include a driver’s license issued by another state or a foreign passport that has been stamped by the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services. It’s vital that the name on your ID matches the name printed on the legal documents exactly. If your name has changed due to marriage or a legal decree but your ID has not been updated, the signing may be delayed.

If a signer lacks a valid photo ID, California allows for the use of “Credible Witnesses.” This process requires two individuals who personally know the signer and have their own valid government-issued IDs to vouch for the signer’s identity under oath. Both witnesses must be present during the appointment and must not have a financial interest in the document being signed.
