Every loan signing agent is a commissioned notary, but not every notary is trained to handle loan documents. A California apostille is issued by the Secretary of State to authenticate a document for use in another country. Most documents are first notarized, then submitted to the state for the apostille. We facilitate the full process: standard mailed filing is $150 (5–10 business days), and same-day counter filing in Sacramento is $199, all state fees included.

In California a power of attorney generally must be either notarized or signed by two adult witnesses, and notarization is required for a POA that will be recorded for real-estate matters. Notarization is the standard, widely accepted choice. We are not attorneys and cannot give legal advice, so confirm your document's specific requirements with your legal advisor.
