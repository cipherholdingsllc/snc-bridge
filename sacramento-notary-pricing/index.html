How Our Pricing Works

One formula for everyone. The visit fee covers travel and scheduling. The notary fee is $15 per notarized signature, set by California law (Gov. Code §8211). Keeping the two separate is how the California Secretary of State says it should be handled, and it means you can see exactly what you pay for. Your total is quoted before we leave.

Same-day costs the same as booking ahead. If you need us sooner, a Priority visit means we leave within the hour, for $25 more.

Which Visit Do You Need?

Standard Documents

Affidavit, vehicle title, financial power of attorney at home or the office, 8 AM–8 PM: a Standard visit. $55 visit fee, so one signature is $70 and two are $85. Same-day included.

Need Us Within the Hour

Closing deadline, last-minute title, travel tomorrow: a Priority visit, where we leave within the hour. $80 visit fee: $95 with one signature.

Evenings and Overnight

Evening visits (8–10 PM) are a $95 visit fee: from $110 with one signature. Overnight (10 PM–8 AM) is by request, $150 visit fee: from $165 with one signature.

Hospital, Hospice or Care Facility

Hospital, hospice, skilled nursing or assisted living: $150 with up to two signatures, $15 each additional. See the hospital bedside page for how bedside visits work.

Jail or Detention

Main Jail and RCCC visits are $250 all-inclusive. See the jail notary page for facility rules.

Wills, Trusts and Estate Documents

Trust, pour-over will, POA, healthcare directive: visit fee + $15 per signature, capped at $175 for up to two signers. Bedside estate signings (hospital, hospice, skilled nursing) use the $150 bedside rate, not the $175 home cap. A standalone power of attorney at home or office is $100 with up to two signatures, then $15 each.

Real Estate / Loan

Flat packages with all notarizations included: Seller $200 · HELOC $200 · Refinance $250 · Purchase $250. We're a certified Notary Signing Agent, background-screened for lender and title approval.

Documents Going Abroad

California apostille, all state fees included. Standard, mailed to the Secretary of State: $150, 5–10 business days. Same-day counter filing in Sacramento: $199; we file the day your document reaches us if it arrives by noon. Return shipping is extra. Each additional document is $60. Documents that must be notarized first (POA, affidavit, diploma) add the standard mobile visit, or $15 per signature if you mail a signed document to us. A POA visit plus apostille is priced as the two services. See the apostille page.

What Every Mobile Visit Includes

Phone Confirmation

We confirm the price and time before anyone drives out, along with the location and ID requirements.

Journal Entry

California law requires a notary journal entry for every act. Ours are complete, timestamped, and retained per state requirements.

ID Verification

We verify identity against current, valid government-issued photo ID. Credible-witness arrangements available when ID is unavailable.

Careful Handling

Documents handled with professional care, reviewed for completeness before any signature goes in the journal.

Supplies Brought to You

We bring the seal, journal, and any required California acknowledgment or jurat forms to your location.

More Pricing Questions

What if I have multiple signatures?

Each notarized signature is $15, set by California law, on top of the visit fee. We count them with you before we leave, so the total is confirmed upfront.

What if you can't complete the notarization?

If we arrive and cannot proceed (signer unavailable or not alert, missing ID) and we have not yet started, there is no charge. We'll explain why and what alternatives exist.

Is the loan signing rate negotiable for bulk orders?

Signing agents working with title companies and escrow can call (916) 302-7057 to discuss volume arrangements.

Can I book in advance?

Yes, any day of the week. Call (916) 302-7057 or use the form below. Same-day visits are the standard rate.
