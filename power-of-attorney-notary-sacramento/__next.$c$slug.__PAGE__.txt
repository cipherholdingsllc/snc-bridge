18:T153b,<h2>Same-Day Mobile Notary for California Powers of Attorney</h2>
<p>A Power of Attorney (POA) often cannot wait for a weekday bank appointment. Families call us when a parent at <a href="/uc-davis-medical-center-notary-sacramento/">UC Davis Medical Center</a>, <a href="/sutter-health-notary-sacramento/">Sutter Medical Center</a>, or <a href="/kaiser-permanente-notary-sacramento/">Kaiser Permanente</a> needs a durable financial or healthcare POA executed before a procedure, when an estate planning attorney needs an in-home signing in <a href="/elk-grove-mobile-notary/">Elk Grove</a> or <a href="/folsom-mobile-notary/">Folsom</a>, or when an international property transaction requires a notarized and <a href="/apostille-sacramento/">apostilled Power of Attorney</a>.</p>
<p>Sacramento Notary Co travels to homes, law offices, hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, and senior living communities across the Sacramento metro area <strong>8 AM to 10 PM, seven days a week</strong>. Home or office POA appointments are <strong>$100 with up to two signatures, then $15 each</strong>, <a href="/hospital-bedside/">hospital and care-facility bedside POA visits</a> are <strong>$150 with up to two signatures, then $15 each</strong>, and a <strong>POA visit + apostille</strong> is priced as the two services.</p>
<h2>California Legal Requirements for Notarizing a Power of Attorney</h2>
<p>Under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&amp;sectionNum=4121." rel="noopener">California Probate Code §4121</a>, a Uniform Statutory Form Power of Attorney (<a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PROB&amp;sectionNum=4401." rel="noopener">Probate Code §4401</a>) or durable financial POA is legally valid when the date of execution is acknowledged before a notary public (or signed by at least two qualified adult witnesses, though banks, title companies, and county recorders require a notary acknowledgment Whenever real property is involved). Every POA notarization in California requires three statutory safeguards:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Personal appearance, awareness, and willingness:</strong> The principal (the person granting authority) must appear in person before the notary, understand what document they are signing, and sign willingly without coercion. For medical or memory-care patients, we coordinate with the family and nursing staff to arrive during the signer’s clearest time of day.</li>
<li><strong>Mandatory right-thumbprint in the notary journal:</strong> Under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&amp;sectionNum=8206." rel="noopener">California Government Code §8206(a)(2)(G)</a>, California law requires the notary public to capture the signer’s right thumbprint in the sequential notarial journal for every Power of Attorney, alongside every deed, quitclaim deed, and deed of trust.</li>
<li><strong>Current government-issued photo ID or two credible witnesses:</strong> The principal presents a valid driver’s license, state ID, or passport issued within the last five years (<a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&amp;sectionNum=1185." rel="noopener">Civil Code §1185</a>). When a hospitalized or elderly principal’s ID is expired or unavailable, two neutral credible witnesses who personally know the signer, carry their own valid photo ID, and have no financial interest in or named role under the POA can establish identity under Civil Code §1185(b)(2).</li>
</ul>
<h2>Types of Powers of Attorney We Notarize in Sacramento</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>California Uniform Statutory Form Power of Attorney (Probate Code §4401):</strong> Covers real property, banking, retirement accounts, tax matters, and personal management.</li>
<li><strong>Attorney-Drafted Durable &amp; Springing Financial POAs:</strong> Executed standalone or as part of a full <a href="/wills-trusts-notary/">living trust and estate signing (capped at $175 for up to two signers)</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Advance Health Care Directives &amp; Medical POAs (Probate Code §4673):</strong> Note that when signed in a <a href="/hospice-notary/">skilled nursing facility</a>, California Probate Code §4675 also requires a Long-Term Care Patient Advocate / Ombudsman witness in addition to notarization.</li>
<li><strong>Real Estate Limited Power of Attorney:</strong> Used for <a href="/loan-signing-agent-sacramento/">real estate closings</a> when one spouse or co-borrower is traveling or deployed.</li>
<li><strong>International Power of Attorney (<em>Carta Poder / Procuração / Poder Notarial</em>):</strong> Notarized in California and filed with the California Secretary of State in Sacramento for a <a href="/same-day-apostille-sacramento/">Hague Apostille</a>.</li>
</ul>
