Why Diplomas Need Notarization Before Apostille

Unlike birth certificates issued with an official government seal, diplomas and transcripts carry a school official’s signature — not a government seal. The California Secretary of State cannot apostille a private school document directly. It must first be notarized by a California notary public, who certifies the signature or produces a certified copy. Sacramento Notary Co handles both steps.

Step 1 · Notarization

We notarize your original diploma, transcript, or a certified copy of it. The notary’s signature and stamp are what the SOS will apostille.

Step 2 · SOS Filing

We file the notarized document at the California Secretary of State Apostille Unit. Standard 5–10 business days; same-day counter filing ($199) available if your document reaches us by noon.

Step 3 · Return

Your apostilled document ships back via USPS Priority or FedEx with full tracking. International return available on request.

Common Uses for Diploma Apostille

Employment Visas

UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and other Gulf states require apostilled academic credentials for professional license registration and work visa applications.

Professional Licensing

Medical, legal, and engineering licensing boards in Germany, South Korea, India, and Brazil require apostilled degrees from US institutions.

University Admissions

Graduate programs in Europe and Asia often require apostilled transcripts from the undergraduate institution as part of the application package.

Immigration

Skilled worker immigration programs in Canada, Australia, and New Zealand may require apostilled credentials for points-based assessments.

Teaching Abroad

Teaching certification programs in South Korea, Japan, China, and the Middle East require apostilled degrees and transcripts alongside teaching credentials.

Business Permits

Opening a professional practice or registering a business in some countries requires apostilled proof of educational qualifications.

Diploma Apostille FAQ

Does a diploma need notarization first?

Yes. Unlike birth certificates, diplomas carry a school official’s signature. A California notary must certify the signature before the SOS can issue an apostille.

How much does it cost?

Standard: $150 per document. Same-day filing: $199. Each additional document: $60. Notarization adds the standard mobile visit ($55 + $15 per signature), or $15 per signature by mail.

How long does it take?

Standard 5–10 business days from receipt. Same-day filing if your document reaches us by noon.

Can I mail my diploma?

Yes. Start your order online or by phone. We'll email your mailing instructions and a prepaid return label option within one business day.

What if I don’t have my original diploma?

Contact your school for a new certified copy or a registrar letter. We can notarize the registrar’s letter as an alternative.

Does apostille work in non-Hague countries?

No. Non-Hague countries (the UAE, Qatar, Egypt) require full legalization through the embassy — we advise on the correct channel.
