<h2>How Our Escrow-to-Table Workflow Prevents Funding Delays</h2>
<p>A single missed initial, undated Notice of Right to Cancel, or smudged notary seal can push a Sacramento County or Placer County recording past the daily cutoff and trigger per-diem interest penalties. Our closing protocol checks every high-failure point at the table before we leave:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dual-copy laser printing, up to 20 pages included:</strong> We print two complete sets on letter and legal stock as required — one wet-signed original package for escrow/lender return and one borrower copy to keep at the table.</li>
<li><strong>Page-by-page signature, initial, and date audit:</strong> We walk borrowers through the Note, Closing Disclosure (CD), Deed of Trust, Occupancy Affidavit, Signature/Name Affidavit, and IRS Form 4506-C, verifying every signature line and borrower date format before packing the file.</li>
<li><strong>California notarial certificate compliance:</strong> All acknowledgments and jurats are completed under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&amp;sectionNum=1189." rel="noopener">California Civil Code §1189</a> and <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&amp;sectionNum=8202." rel="noopener">Government Code §8202</a>, including mandatory right-thumbprint journal entries for every deed, quitclaim deed, and deed of trust under <a href="https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&amp;sectionNum=8206." rel="noopener">Government Code §8206(a)(2)(G)</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Same-day scan-back and FedEx/UPS drop:</strong> When escrow requests scan-backs for pre-funding review, we transmit high-resolution PDFs within 60 minutes of table completion and drop the physical package before the last air courier pickup or hand-deliver to local Sacramento metro title offices.</li>
</ul>
