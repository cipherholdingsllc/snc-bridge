Mobile Notary Dispatch Across Roseville & South Placer County

Roseville sits along the I-80 and Highway 65 corridors in Placer County, where medical center visits, Sun City estate signings, and West Roseville residential closings each follow distinct schedules. Sacramento Notary Co dispatches directly to homes, corporate campuses, hospital rooms, and senior living communities across all three Roseville ZIP codes — 95661, 95678, and 95747 — seven days a week from 8 AM to 10 PM.

Standard Roseville mobile appointments are a $55 visit fee plus $15 per notarized signature ($70 with one signature), plus $25 Roseville travel quoted upfront before we leave. Same-day is the standard rate. Hospital bedside signings at Sutter Roseville Medical Center or Kaiser Roseville are $150 with up to two signatures, then $15 each, living trust and estate signings are capped at $175 for up to two signers, and real estate loan signings run $200 to $250 flat depending on package type.
